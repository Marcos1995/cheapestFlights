## Problem Statement

Google Flights, Skyscanner, and Kayak show the obvious itinerary. They under-rank (or hide) the options a price-sensitive leisure traveler actually wants:

1. **Tarifa error** — a published fare far below the route's normal price (currency glitch, missing surcharge, filing typo). Lasts hours.
2. **Ruta rara que sí vuelas** — extra stop, nearby airport, or self-transfer that is cheaper than the "simple" ticket, and you take every segment.
3. **Ciudad oculta** — want Rome (FCO). Book BCN→RGS (Burgos) with a connection at FCO, get off in Rome, skip Burgos. Cheaper, but it violates airline contracts of carriage.

The founder asked whether this is a real idea. It is a real *category*. It is not a real *v1* if v1 is "Skyscanner plus extras."

## Demand Evidence

None beyond founder-as-user. The only evidence in this session is a hypothetical: Barcelona–Rome "should" be €200, a longer ticket via Rome to Burgos might be €100–150.

That is a workflow description, not demand. Existing products (Going, Secret Flying, FlightGlitch, Skiplagged, Kiwi) prove *someone* wants pieces of this. They do not prove *this founder* can get those people to switch.

**Position:** treat demand as unproven. The prototype exists to measure it, not to assume it.

## Status Quo

- Normal trip: search Google Flights / Skyscanner / Kayak, pick the top result, book.
- Error fares: follow Going, Secret Flying, FlightGlitch. Speed-to-alert is the product.
- Hidden city: Skiplagged.
- Creative routing / self-transfer: Kiwi.com.

Cost of the workaround: 20–40 minutes of tab-juggling. Most people just overpay and fly direct.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

A Barcelona leisure traveler who will add 3+ hours of travel to save €50–150, carry-on only, flying 2–6 times a year to Rome / Paris / London / Amsterdam.

v1: BCN-origin one-way search, three labeled buckets, "frente al vuelo simple" savings. No booking. No GDS.

## Premises

1. A subset of European leisure travelers will take a worse itinerary to save €50–150. Mass overpay vs Google Flights is unproven.
2. A new global Skyscanner clone dies on data access and CAC.
3. v1 is a few origin airports and "find me cheaper weird options."
4. Hidden-city is labeled, optional, never the brand.
5. Error fares are an alert problem. v1 search ranks routing hacks; alerts are v2.

## Recommended Approach

**A — BCN corridor prototype.** Product name: **Desvío**.

Locked rules, stack, API schema, edge cases, and success bars: see the full office-hours doc in `~/.gstack/projects/cheapestflights/fassb-main-design-20260825-191403.md` (same content as this file's source of truth).

### v1 product rules

- One-way, BCN origin only.
- Destinations: FCO, CDG, LGW, AMS, LIS, ATH, BER, MXP, PRG, VIE.
- Full templates on 5 anchors: FCO, CDG, LGW, AMS, LIS.
- One card per bucket. Mock catalog only.
- Hidden-city: templates only (want FCO → ticket BCN–RGS via FCO).
- Error fares: `/demo/tarifa-error` only, not search results.
- UI Spanish; code English.

## The Assignment

Message 10 people who flew from BCN in the last year. Ask what they paid, whether they would take a 4-hour worse itinerary to save €80, and whether they have used Kiwi or Skiplagged.
